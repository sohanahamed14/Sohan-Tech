⚠️ Beware of Counterfeit Parts

Your safety is our highest priority. Please read this important guidance before purchasing or using replacement parts for your devices.

🔋

Counterfeit Power Adapters & Batteries

Some counterfeit and third-party power adapters and batteries may not be designed properly and could result in serious safety issues — including overheating, fire, or electric shock.

  • Always purchase adapters and batteries from authorised retailers or directly from the manufacturer.
  • If you need a replacement adapter to charge your device, we recommend getting an official power adapter from the original brand.
  • For battery replacements, visit a certified service provider who uses genuine components.
  • If a deal seems too good to be true, it often is — counterfeit parts are commonly sold at suspiciously low prices.
🖥️

Non-Genuine Replacement Displays

Non-genuine replacement displays may have compromised visual quality and may fail to work correctly. This includes issues with:

  • Inaccurate colour reproduction and brightness
  • Touch sensitivity problems or ghost touches
  • Flickering screens and backlight bleed
  • Incompatibility with software-level display features

Screen repairs should be performed by trusted experts who use genuine, certified display panels for your device model.

✅

Our Commitment to Genuine Products

At SOHAN TECH, every product we sell is 100% authentic and sourced directly from authorised distributors and manufacturers. We do not stock, sell, or recommend counterfeit parts — your safety and satisfaction come first.

💡

How to Spot a Counterfeit

  • Price: Genuine parts are rarely heavily discounted — extremely cheap items are often fake.
  • Packaging: Look for spelling mistakes, blurry logos, or flimsy packaging.
  • Weight & Build: Genuine adapters are heavier and feel more solid.
  • Certification marks: Look for official certification labels (BIS, CE, etc.).
  • Seller: Always buy from authorised dealers or brand-official stores.
